# Customer Groups and Rewards: Questions for the Client

A short question sheet for the spa owner, for the open item in [Task 7](../PLANNED_TASKS.md#task-7-make-the-rfm-rules-friendly-for-non-technical-users). It can be printed or read aloud. Write the answers on the lines under each question.

## How Rewards Work Today

The website sorts every customer who has paid for a visit by three things: when they last visited, how often they come, and how much they have spent in total. The site compares each customer with all your other customers, so "recent" or "often" means "more than most of your customers".

Each customer joins the first group they fit, checking the groups from top to bottom. A group can give one free add-on and can recommend a membership card. A customer holds only one unused reward at a time. Rewards expire after 90 days unless you choose 30, 60 or 180 days, or no expiry.

## The Five Groups Today

| Group | Who is in it | Free add-on | Card offered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High-value customer | Visited recently, comes often and spends the most | VIP Room (₱200) | Platinum |
| Loyal customer | Comes often and still visits fairly recently | 30-Minute Back Massage (₱299) | Gold |
| At-risk customer | Used to come often but has not been back lately | Ventosa (₱200) | None |
| Inactive customer | Has not visited in the longest time | Hot Stone (₱200) | None |
| New customer | Came once, very recently | Hot Compress (₱200) | None |

## What Happens With the Answers

- Group names, descriptions, free add-ons, group order, reward validity and membership cards can all be changed on the admin pages, with no programming. We will make those changes with you.
- Two kinds of answer would be new work, recorded in [Planned Tasks](../PLANNED_TASKS.md) first: grouping customers by something other than visits and spending, or using fixed limits (such as "60 days without a visit") instead of comparing customers with each other.
