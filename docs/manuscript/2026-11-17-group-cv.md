## Format

Based on the sample CV supplied by the user, adapted for students. The tab opens with a highlighted draft note, then one CV per member, each starting on its own page inside a one-cell table with a 1.5 pt border and a minimum height of one page. Moving a CV means copying that one box.

Sections, in order: 2x2 photo, name, contact lines; Summary; Experience (the Casa Paraiso capstone role first, then OJT or other work); Education (college, senior high school, junior high school); Awards and Honors; Trainings, Workshops and Seminars; Technical Skills; Character References; the certification line with name and "Proponent".

Text is Times New Roman 11 pt, single-spaced, section headings 12 pt bold with a rule underneath. Placeholders are in [brackets]. The shared facts (degree, university, capstone title) are already filled in.

## Questions for the group

Copy and send this to the group chat:

> Hi! For the Curriculum Vitae in our manuscript, please answer the following before November 19 so we can finish it by November 20. Write "none" if an item does not apply.
>
> **1. Personal details**
> - Full name, with middle initial (as it should appear on the CV)
> - Mobile number
> - Email address (preferably a professional one)
> - Facebook, LinkedIn or GitHub link (optional)
> - Home address (city and province is enough)
> - Birthdate, age, sex, civil status and nationality (only if we decide to include a Personal Information section)
> - A 2x2 photo: formal attire, plain white background, recent
>
> **2. Summary**
> - In 2 to 3 sentences: your strongest skills, what you are good at, and the kind of work or career you want after graduation
>
> **3. Capstone project role (Casa Paraiso)**
> - Your role or title in the project (for example, programmer, front-end developer, documentation, QA, project manager)
> - The month and year you started and finished
> - 2 to 3 specific things you did (modules, features, chapters, testing, client meetings)
>
> **4. Work experience and OJT**
> - OJT or internship: company name, address, position, start and end dates, 2 to 3 duties or accomplishments
> - Any part-time, freelance or other work, with the same details
> - Organization or council positions held, with dates
>
> **5. Education**
> - College: year you entered and expected graduation year
> - Senior high school: school name, city, strand, year graduated, honors received
> - Junior high school: school name, city, year graduated, honors received
>
> **6. Awards and honors**
> - Title, who gave it, and month and year (Dean's lister, academic awards, competitions, certificates of recognition)
>
> **7. Trainings, workshops, seminars and certifications**
> - Title, organizer, venue or "online", and date (webinars, TESDA or NC certificates, online course certificates)
>
> **8. Skills**
> - Programming languages, frameworks, databases and tools you can use
> - Soft skills (for example, teamwork, communication, leadership)
> - Languages you speak
>
> **9. Character references (2 to 3, not relatives)**
> - Full name, position, company or school, and contact number or email
> - Please ask their permission first

## Follow-ups

- Decide as a group whether to add a Personal Information section (birthdate, sex, civil status, nationality); the template leaves it out.
- With real content, a CV may run past one page; the box then continues onto the next page. Shorten the bullets or ask the adviser whether two pages are allowed.
- Photos must be inserted by hand in Google Docs; the connector can only insert images from a public URL.
