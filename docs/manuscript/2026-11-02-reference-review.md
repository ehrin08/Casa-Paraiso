# Casa Paraiso manuscript: finished-paper review

**Verdict:** The manuscript has the five-chapter foundation and a broadly matching academic layout, but it is not yet a finished submission. Its most important gaps are incomplete front/back matter, stale navigation lists, outdated illustrations, and unresolved evaluation evidence. Formatting alone will not close the evidence gaps.

This is a separate review, not a revision or merger. Neither manuscript was changed.

## What should not be copied from the reference

Do not import LipaGo's topic, objectives, transport modules, datasets, model, metrics, participants, scores, bibliography, people or approvals. Its contents also contain errors: acknowledgments and contents page references are shifted, its listed dedication is not a distinct visible page, and its contents place evaluation results under Chapter V while the inspected body presents them in Chapter IV. Use its broad presentation pattern, not its inconsistencies.

The most useful finished-paper pattern is: clear front matter; a complete and accurate navigation list; methods tied to objectives; readable current diagrams and screenshots; separately evidenced technical and user evaluations; conclusions bounded by those results; and original supporting appendices.
